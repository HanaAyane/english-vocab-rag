☀ <span class="category">**改变方向：**</span>

<span class="vocabulary">**swerve**</span> [swɜːrv]
<span class="definition">vi. 突然转向，常见于急忙躲避障碍物：</span>The rider swerved to avoid a fallen branch. 骑车人猛地转弯，避开一根掉落的树枝。

<span class="vocabulary">**veer**</span> [vɪr]
<span class="definition">vi. 偏离原来的方向：</span>The boat veered toward the pier. 小船偏向了码头。

<span class="vocabulary">**bend**</span> [bend]
<span class="definition">n. 道路或河流的弯曲处：</span>There is a narrow bridge just past the bend. 拐过弯不远就是一座窄桥。
